ENERGY ECONOMICS

Why Air-Source Heat Pumps?

Conventional electrical geysers convert 1 kW of electricity into 1 kW of heat. VINDSOL heat pumps transfer ambient environmental heat into water, delivering 3 to 5+ kW of thermal energy for every 1 kW consumed.
