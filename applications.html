RESIDENTIAL

Luxury Villas & Apartments

High-pressure monoblock heat pump systems coupled with 300L to 750L insulated storage tanks. Replaces multiple noisy power-guzzling wall geysers with a single centralized quiet outdoor unit (<48 dBA).

Hotels, Resorts & Clubhouses

Continuous central hot water loops for 50 to 300+ guest rooms, laundry, and banquet kitchens. Eliminates expensive diesel boiler combustion and cuts monthly utility bills by 70% to 75%.
