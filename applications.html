Target Application Segments

Custom hydraulic heating solutions engineered for Indian residential, hospitality, healthcare, and commercial building infrastructure.

RESIDENTIAL

Luxury Villas & Apartments

High-pressure monoblock heat pump systems coupled with 300L to 750L insulated storage tanks. Replaces multiple noisy power-guzzling wall geysers with a single centralized quiet outdoor unit (<48 dBA).

Recommended Unit:

VDHP 6000 MB / VDHP 11000 MB (3.1 kW to 11 kW output, COP 4.9x–5.8x)

HOSPITALITY

Hotels, Resorts & Clubhouses

Continuous central hot water loops for 50 to 300+ guest rooms, laundry, and banquet kitchens. Eliminates expensive diesel boiler combustion and cuts monthly utility bills by 70% to 75%.

Recommended Unit:

VCHP 1720 / VCHP 3500 V-Type Modular Cascade (17 kW to 70 kW)

HEALTHCARE

Hospitals & Medical Centers

Strict 60°C–65°C hot water temperature maintenance required for patient sanitation, surgical sterilization pre-heating, and hydrotherapy pools. Includes 24/7 SLA emergency service support.

Recommended Unit:

Vindsol High-Temp EVI Series (Hot water output up to 75°C)

Handling massive morning peak water demand (6 AM to 9 AM) for 500+ students. Automated timer schedules start heating early morning while Wi-Fi app monitors tank status remotely.
