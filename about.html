MAINTENANCE & LONGEVITY

Why do I need to service / maintain my Heat Pump?

"You service your car, why not your heat pump? Once your new heat pump is installed it is important to keep it running at optimal performance."

Basic Owner Checklist

As an owner, you shall include a few basic things like cleaning the air intake side of your heat pump, and cleaning and removal of any obstructions on the fan side.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Please ensure the system is OFF when you do these basic things by yourself.

Expert Maintenance Program

To ease it out, you shall opt for our service department staffed with expert technicians who handle your service and yearly maintenance calls. Ask about our Maintenance Program to ensure optimal efficiency and operation of your new heat pump.

Debris & Coil Cleaning Science

Over time your heat pump will start to build up debris and dander on both the indoor and outdoor coils that will reduce efficiency and shorten the life of the unit if not cleaned and serviced. A heat pump is constantly pulling air (and anything in it) over both the indoor and outdoor coils and as such debris builds up over time that needs to be cleaned out (like an air filter on a car).
